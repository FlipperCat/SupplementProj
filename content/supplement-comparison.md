## What the Comparison Tool does

Pick any two (or three) options and the tool puts them side-by-side on the things that actually matter:

- **Bioavailability** — how much of the dose your body can actually use.
- **Best-for goal** — which form has the strongest evidence for sleep, energy, anxiety, performance, etc.
- **Side effect profile** — GI tolerance, common reactions, who should avoid.
- **Cost per effective dose** (not per pill — per *usable* dose).
- **Third-party testing status** — NSF, USP, Informed Sport, or none.

It works two ways: **compare forms of the same supplement** (Magnesium Glycinate vs. Citrate vs. Threonate vs. Oxide), or **compare brands** of the same form (Thorne Glycinate vs. Doctor's Best vs. NOW).

## How to use it

1. **Pick a category** at the top (e.g., Magnesium, Omega-3, Probiotics).
2. **Select 2 or 3 options** to compare. The tool will surface the forms or brands most worth comparing.
3. **Set your goal** if it's goal-specific (sleep vs. constipation vs. exercise performance change which form wins).
4. **Scan the comparison table** — winners on each criterion are highlighted.
5. **Click any row** for the underlying detail (study citations, typical dose, etc.).

## Examples

**Example — Magnesium forms compared for sleep**

| | Glycinate | Citrate | Threonate | Oxide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bioavailability** | High | Medium-High | Medium (brain-specific) | Very low (~4%) |
| **Best for** | Sleep, anxiety, muscle relaxation | Constipation, general | Cognition, memory | Almost nothing useful |
| **GI side effects** | Minimal | Loose stools at higher doses | Minimal | Common |
| **Cost / effective dose** | $$ | $ | $$$ | $ (but mostly wasted) |
| **Winner for sleep?** | ✅ | — | (brain-specific, not sedating) | ❌ |

Practical takeaway: for sleep, **Glycinate**. For chronic constipation, **Citrate**. For cognition (separate question), **Threonate**. Avoid **Oxide** for anything but cheap laxative effect.

**Example — Fish oil brands compared for cardiovascular**

Comparing three popular Omega-3s at 1 g EPA+DHA per day:
- *Brand A (premium):* Independently tested for oxidation, IFOS 5-star, ~$1.20/serving.
- *Brand B (mid-tier):* USP-verified, no public oxidation test, ~$0.60/serving.
- *Brand C (cheap):* No third-party testing, oxidation tests show rancidity in independent reviews. ~$0.30/serving.

The tool flags Brand C as a hard no — rancid fish oil is not just ineffective, it's *pro-inflammatory* (the opposite of why you're taking it). Brand B is the value pick. Brand A is the right call if you're taking high doses (3+ g/day) or want maximum testing.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>How do you decide which form "wins"?</summary>
Each comparison is goal-anchored. Magnesium for sleep, Citrate for constipation, Threonate for cognition — they're all "good" forms in their lane and not interchangeable. The winner is whichever form has the strongest evidence for the *specific goal* you selected.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the cheapest option always bad?</summary>
No. For many supplements (Creatine Monohydrate, Vitamin D3, basic Magnesium Glycinate), the cheap version *is* the right version — the molecule is the molecule. The expensive option is worth it when (a) the form genuinely matters, or (b) third-party testing matters for your situation (athletes subject to drug testing, people with sensitivities, anyone stacking 10+ supplements).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between this and the Cost Calculator?</summary>
The Comparison Tool helps you **pick the right form/brand** for a single supplement. The <a href="/cost-calculator/">Cost Calculator</a> takes your final picks and prices out your whole monthly stack. Use Comparison first, then Cost Calculator.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why aren't all brands listed?</summary>
We only include brands that pass a curation filter: no FDA warning letters, no documented fake-review patterns, no repeated independent testing failures. That removes a lot of Amazon-only brands. If a brand you trust isn't listed, let us know via the <a href="/contact/">contact page</a>.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I compare different supplements (not just different forms)?</summary>
For "X vs Y as a sleep aid" type questions (e.g., Magnesium vs Melatonin vs Ashwagandha), check the goal-specific guides in the <a href="/goals/">Goals section</a> — those compare across categories. This tool is built for in-category comparisons.
</details>
