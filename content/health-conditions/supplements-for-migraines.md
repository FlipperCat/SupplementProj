## Migraines & Supplements: Prevention vs Relief

Migraine supplements work through two mechanisms:
1. **Prevention** - Reduce migraine frequency/severity over time
2. **Acute relief** - Reduce pain/symptoms during active migraine

---

## Preventive Supplements (Daily)

### Magnesium (Most Important)

**Why essential:**
- 50% of migraineurs deficient
- Magnesium regulates neurotransmitters (serotonin, dopamine)
- Prevents vascular changes leading to migraines
- Strongest supplement evidence for migraine prevention

**Dose:** 400-500 mg daily (glycinate or malate form)

**Timeline:** 4-12 weeks to notice reduction in migraines

**Cost:** $8-12/month

**Best form:** Magnesium glycinate (gentler than oxide)

**Evidence:** 200+ studies; FDA-considered for migraine prevention

---

### CoQ10

**Why it works:**
- Reduces migraine frequency
- Improves mitochondrial function (migraines linked to mitochondrial dysfunction)
- Safe long-term use
- Research-backed specifically for migraines

**Dose:** 150-300 mg daily

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks

**Cost:** $15-25/month

**Best form:** Ubiquinol (absorbed better than ubiquinone)

---

### Riboflavin (Vitamin B2)

**Why it works:**
- Supports mitochondrial energy
- Reduces migraine frequency significantly
- Often given in medical clinics for migraines
- Safe, water-soluble

**Dose:** 400 mg daily

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks

**Cost:** $5-10/month

**Note:** High-dose B2; basic B-complex dosing insufficient

---

### Omega-3 (Fish Oil)

**Why it works:**
- Reduces inflammation
- Supports blood vessel health
- Decreases migraine frequency
- Supports neurovascular regulation

**Dose:** 2-3 grams EPA+DHA daily

**Timeline:** 4-12 weeks for migraine reduction

**Cost:** $12-20/month

---

### Butterbur (Petasites)

**Why it works:**
- Reduces migraine frequency specifically
- Works on vascular component of migraines
- Research specifically supports migraine prevention
- Well-tolerated

**Dose:** 75 mg extract daily (PA-free form only)

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks

**Cost:** $15-25/month

**Critical:** Must be PA-free (pyrrolizidine alkaloid-free) to avoid liver toxicity

---

### Feverfew

**Why it works:**
- Traditional migraine prevention herb
- Reduces inflammation and vascular reactivity
- May reduce migraine frequency
- Generally safe long-term

**Dose:** 75-150 mg daily (standardized to 0.4% parthenolide)

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks

**Cost:** $10-15/month

---

## Acute Migraine Relief

### Ginger Extract (During Migraine)

**Why it works:**
- Reduces pain as effectively as sumatriptan (research shows)
- Anti-inflammatory
- Reduces nausea (common with migraines)
- Safe to use during acute migraine

**Dose:** 500-1,000 mg at migraine onset

**Timeline:** 30-60 minutes for pain relief

**Cost:** Included in monthly budget

**Form:** Extract (more potent than dried ginger)

---

### Magnesium (Acute)

**Why it helps:**
- IV magnesium used in hospitals for acute migraines
- Oral form helps if you're chronically deficient
- Reduces pain intensity
- Supports recovery

**Dose:** 500 mg at migraine onset (additional to daily dose)

**Timeline:** 30-60 minutes

**Cost:** Included in monthly budget

---

### Riboflavin (Acute)

**Why it helps:**
- High-dose B2 during acute migraine
- Supports energy production
- Reduces pain intensity

**Dose:** 400 mg at onset (additional to daily dose)

**Timeline:** 1-2 hours

**Cost:** Included in monthly budget

---

### Peppermint Oil (Topical)

**Why it works:**
- Apply to temples/forehead
- Cooling/soothing effect
- Reduces tension
- Can combine with oral supplements

**Application:** Topical to temples (diluted in carrier oil)

**Timeline:** 15-30 minutes

**Cost:** $5-10/bottle (lasts months)

---

## Preventive Stack

### Essential Prevention Stack ($50-80/month)
- Magnesium glycinate: 400-500 mg daily
- CoQ10: 150 mg daily
- Riboflavin (B2): 400 mg daily
- Omega-3: 2-3g daily

### Optimal Prevention Stack ($90-140/month)
- All above, plus:
- Butterbur extract: 75 mg daily
- Feverfew: 75-100 mg daily
- Extra magnesium (malate form for energy): 150 mg

### Acute Relief During Migraine
- Ginger extract: 500-1,000 mg at onset
- Extra magnesium: 500 mg at onset
- Extra riboflavin: 400 mg at onset
- Peppermint oil: Topical as needed

---

## Sample Daily Protocol

### Morning
- Magnesium glycinate: 200 mg
- Riboflavin (B2): 400 mg (take with food)
- CoQ10: 150 mg (take with fat)
- Omega-3: 1-1.5g

### Evening
- Magnesium glycinate: 200 mg
- Omega-3: 1-1.5g
- Butterbur: 75 mg
- Feverfew: 75 mg

### During Migraine
- Ginger extract: 500-1,000 mg
- Magnesium: 500 mg (additional)
- Peppermint oil: Apply topically
- Rest in dark room

## Realistic Expectations

**With preventive stack:**
- Migraine frequency reduction: 30-50% in 4-8 weeks
- Pain intensity reduction: 20-30%
- Duration reduction: 10-20% shorter migraines

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks to notice frequency reduction; 12 weeks for full effect

**Individual variation:** Some respond excellently (60%+ reduction); some see minimal effect (genetics)

---

## What Doesn't Work (Or Limited Evidence)

❌ **Limited/no evidence:**
- Ginseng
- Valerian
- Passionflower
- Most other herbs (not researched for migraines)

**Note:** Just because not researched doesn't mean harmful; just means evidence lacking

---

## Migraine Triggers to Address

**Supplements help BUT also need to:**
- Identify triggers (caffeine, alcohol, MSG, skipped meals, stress, sleep, hormonal)
- Manage stress (meditation, exercise)
- Sleep consistently
- Stay hydrated
- Avoid trigger foods
- Manage hormonal factors (if applicable)

**Best results:** Supplements + lifestyle + medical management (if on preventive medications)

---

## Drug Interactions

**Check with doctor if on:**
- Blood thinners (omega-3, ginger may increase bleeding)
- Migraine medications (most supplements safe but verify)
- Blood pressure drugs (CoQ10 may enhance effects)

**Generally safe with:**
- Most migraine abortives (triptans, etc.)
- Preventive medications
- Most common drugs

---

## Bottom Line

**Migraine supplementation focuses on: prevention through mitochondrial support and vascular health.**

**Most critical (Prevention):**
1. **Magnesium** (most important; 400-500 mg daily)
2. **CoQ10** (mitochondrial support; 150 mg daily)
3. **Riboflavin B2** (energy; 400 mg daily)
4. **Omega-3** (vascular health; 2-3g daily)

**For Acute Relief:**
1. **Ginger** (pain relief; 500-1,000 mg at onset)
2. **Extra Magnesium** (500 mg at onset)
3. **Peppermint oil** (topical cooling)

**Key takeaways:**
- Prevention: 4-12 weeks to see results
- Expect: 30-50% migraine frequency reduction
- Cost: $50-150/month depending on stack
- Best evidence: Magnesium, CoQ10, Riboflavin, Butterbur
- Add: Medical management + lifestyle changes for best results

**The reality:** Supplements help prevent migraines but work best alongside medications and lifestyle management. Individual response varies significantly.
