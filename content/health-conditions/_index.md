## Important Notes

- **Always consult your doctor** before starting supplements for any health condition
- **Check medication interactions** using our [Medication Interaction Checker](/medication-checker/)
- **Start low, go slow** — introduce one supplement at a time to identify what works
- **These are complementary** — supplements support, not replace, medical treatment
