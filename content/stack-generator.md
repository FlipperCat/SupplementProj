## Customizing Your Stack

**Want to combine stacks?**
- Safe to combine stacks from different goals (Energy + Sleep, Muscle + Joint health, etc.)
- Check for overlapping supplements (avoid doubling up)
- Adjust total budget accordingly

**Want to modify for budget?**
- Start with Budget tier
- Add 1-2 supplements at a time from Optimal
- Build up to Premium as budget allows

**Want medication-safe stacks?**
- Use our **[Medication Interaction Checker](/medication-checker/)**
- We flag all problematic interactions
- Many supplements work fine with medications (just need proper spacing)
