## Understanding Supplements

### **Supplement Forms: Does It Matter?**

Yes! The form affects bioavailability (how much your body absorbs):

- **Collagen:** Peptides > Powder > Whole (peptides absorb better)
- **Magnesium:** Glycinate (gentle), Threonate (brain), Citrate (laxative)
- **Vitamin B12:** Methylcobalamin > Cyanocobalamin
- **Iron:** Chelated > Iron salts (less GI upset)
- **Vitamin D:** Liquid > Capsules (faster absorption)

**[See detailed form comparisons →](/guides/supplement-forms-bioavailability/)**

### **Bioavailability: Not All Supplements Are Equal**

Two products with the same ingredients can have vastly different absorption:

- **Cheap brands:** Low bioavailability (body doesn't absorb well)
- **Mid-range brands:** Moderate bioavailability (decent effectiveness)
- **Premium brands:** High bioavailability (body absorbs 80%+)

**The difference can be 2-3x** between cheap and quality products.

## Before You Buy

**3 Important Questions:**

1. **Is it tested?** - Look for USP, NSF, or ConsumerLab certification
2. **What form is it?** - Some forms absorb better; see comparisons
3. **What's the cost-per-dose?** - Higher price ≠ better value

## Red Flags When Buying

🚩 **Avoid These Supplements:**
- Unusually cheap prices (quality compromised)
- No third-party testing listed
- Vague ingredient sourcing
- Excessive marketing claims
- Proprietary blends (you don't know dosages)
- Made in unregulated countries

---

## Shopping Smarter

✓ **Look For:**
- Third-party testing badges (USP, NSF, ConsumerLab)
- Clear ingredient sourcing
- Specific dosages listed (not proprietary blends)
- Manufacturer reputation
- Customer reviews (real feedback)
- Cost-per-dose analysis
