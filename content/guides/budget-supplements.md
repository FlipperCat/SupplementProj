## What NOT to Sacrifice

### Don't Skimp On:
- **Probiotics** (quality matters for strains) - Go budget-friendly brand (Nutricost 50B CFU)
- **Omega-3** (look for mercury testing) - Ensure third-party tested
- **Iron** (if deficient) - Must be effective form
- **B12** (especially on metformin) - Methylated form preferred

### Can Save Money On:
- **Multivitamins** - Budget brands adequate
- **Vitamin D3** - All are vitamin D3 essentially
- **Magnesium** - Glycinate form available budget-friendly
- **Vitamin C** - All ascorbic acid similar

## Generic vs. Brand Name

**Generic Supplements:**
- ✅ Often 50-70% cheaper
- ✅ Same active ingredients as brand name
- ✅ Should have same potency (if third-party tested)
- ✅ Good option for budget shoppers

**Brand Name Supplements:**
- ✅ More research backing some
- ✅ Better marketing/recognition
- ✅ Often better quality control
- ✅ Not always necessary to pay more

**Best Approach:** Buy quality generics (Nutricost, iHerb brands) instead of cheap knock-offs

## Shopping Tips for Maximum Savings

1. **Buy at iHerb** - 10-20% cheaper for most brands
2. **Use Amazon Subscribe & Save** - 5-20% discount
3. **Buy 3-month supply** - Usually 20-40% savings
4. **Wait for sales** - Black Friday especially

6. **Skip fancy packaging** - Pay for supplement, not box
7. **Buy solo supplements** - Cheaper than "stacks"
