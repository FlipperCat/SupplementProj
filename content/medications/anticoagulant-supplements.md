## Critical: Blood Thinners + Supplements

**This is serious territory.** Supplements can significantly interact with anticoagulants, affecting bleeding risk.

**EVERY supplement must be discussed with your cardiologist/hematologist before starting.**

## Blood Thinner Types

**Warfarin (Coumadin)**:
- Vitamin K antagonist
- Requires INR monitoring
- Many supplement interactions

**Direct Oral Anticoagulants (DOACs)**:
- Apixaban (Eliquat)
- Dabigatran (Pradaxa)
- Rivaroxaban (Xarelto)
- Edoxaban (Savaysa)
- Fewer interactions than warfarin

**Enoxaparin, Heparin**:
- Injectable anticoagulants
- Different interaction profile

## Supplements to AVOID

**NEVER take without doctor approval**:
- ❌ St. John's Wort (affects warfarin metabolism)
- ❌ Ginkgo biloba (antiplatelet effect)
- ❌ Ginger (high doses; antiplatelet)
- ❌ Garlic (antiplatelet)
- ❌ Curcumin (mild antiplatelet; discuss)
- ❌ Feverfew (antiplatelet)
- ❌ Dong quai (warfarin interaction)

**CAUTION with high doses**:
- ⚠️ Fish oil/Omega-3 (>3g daily can enhance thinning)
- ⚠️ Vitamin E (high doses; mild thinning effect)
- ⚠️ Aspirin (obviously; check if already prescribed)

## Safe Supplements (Generally)

**With warfarin**:
- ✅ Vitamin C (avoid excessive doses)
- ✅ Magnesium (general support)
- ✅ B-Complex (but AVOID high-dose folate; affects warfarin)
- ✅ CoQ10 (some evidence of consistency; must maintain stable dose)
- ✅ Probiotics (generally safe)

**With DOACs** (Apixaban, Dabigatran, etc.):
- ✅ Most supplements safer than with warfarin
- ✅ Vitamin C, magnesium, B-vitamins generally safe
- ✅ Probiotics safe
- ✅ Even moderate omega-3 (2-3g) usually fine
- ✅ CoQ10 safe

## Supplements Requiring Discussion

**Discuss with cardiologist**:
- Omega-3 (>2g daily discuss)
- CoQ10 (consistent dosing important)
- Vitamin E (high dose discuss)
- Probiotics (generally safe; discuss if new)
- Vitamin K supplements (discuss)

## Critical Rules

### INR Monitoring (Warfarin Only)

**INR = International Normalized Ratio** (measures how thin your blood is)
- Target: Usually 2-3
- Must recheck frequently if adding ANY supplements
- Some supplements shift INR

**If taking warfarin**:
- More frequent INR testing when starting supplements
- Maintain absolutely consistent vitamin K intake (leafy greens)
- Tell hematologist about ALL supplements
- Some supplements may require dose adjustment of warfarin

### Consistency is Key

**For warfarin especially**:
- Once you establish a supplement regimen, NEVER change without telling your doctor
- Even stopping a supplement abruptly can shift INR
- If you want to stop a supplement, discuss with hematologist first
- INR recheck recommended 1 week after any supplement changes

## Safe General Support

### Essential Support for Those on Anticoagulants

**Safe to start (after doctor approval)**:
- B-Complex (B12, folate important for cardiovascular health)
- Magnesium (300-400 mg daily; cardiovascular support)
- Probiotics (general health)
- Vitamin D (if deficient; cardiovascular benefit)

**Cost**: ~$30-50/month

**Timeline**: Ongoing maintenance; recheck INR after starting

## Sample Protocol (Requires Doctor Approval)

### Morning
- B-Complex methylated: Full spectrum
- Magnesium: 150 mg (if split dosing)
- Vitamin D3: 2,000 IU (if deficient)
- Vitamin C: 500 mg (conservative dose)

### Evening
- Magnesium: 150-200 mg
- Probiotics: 15 billion CFU (if 2x daily)

## What NOT to Change

**DO NOT stop or change**:
- Any anticoagulant medication (obviously)
- Stable vitamin K intake if on warfarin (consistency matters more than amount)
- Any supplement without doctor approval

**DO check with your doctor**:
- Any NEW supplement
- ANY herbal products
- Even "natural" products (many have anticoagulant effects)
- Dietary changes (especially if on warfarin; vitamin K changes affect INR)

## When to Seek Emergency Care

**Call 911 or go to ER if**:
- Unusual bleeding or bruising
- Blood in urine or stool
- Severe headache
- Confusion or difficulty speaking
- Chest pain
- Shortness of breath

## The Bottom Line

**On blood thinners, supplementation is conservative, not aggressive.**

**Safe approach**:
1. **Talk to your hematologist/cardiologist** FIRST
2. **Start with basics**: B-Complex, Magnesium, Probiotics
3. **Avoid anticoagulant supplements**: Ginkgo, Ginger, Garlic, Curcumin
4. **Maintain consistency**: Don't change regimen without medical approval
5. **Recheck INR** (if warfarin) 1 week after any changes
6. **Consistency beats optimization**: Stable regimen is better than ideal regimen

**The reality**: You're already on a powerful anticoagulant. Most supplementation adds risk, not benefit. Keep it simple.
