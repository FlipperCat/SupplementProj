## General Guidelines

### **Always Safe (Usually)**
- Magnesium (most medications)
- Omega-3 fish oil (usually, check with doctor if on blood thinners)
- Vitamin C
- B vitamins
- Probiotics

### **Check First (Variable)**
- Calcium (interferes with some antibiotics and thyroid meds)
- Iron (interferes with many medications)
- Vitamin K (problematic if on blood thinners)
- Ginkgo (may increase bleeding risk)
- St. John's Wort (interferes with many drugs)

### **High Risk (Usually Avoid)**
- Supplement herbs that affect drug metabolism
- High-dose vitamin K (if on blood thinners)
- Supplements affecting blood pressure

## Drug Classes & Their Depletions

| Medication Class | Common Depletions | Recommended Support |
|---|---|---|
| **Statins** | CoQ10, B vitamins | CoQ10 100-200mg daily |
| **Blood Pressure Drugs** | CoQ10, magnesium | CoQ10 + Magnesium |
| **Diabetes Medications** | B12, folate, CoQ10 | B-Complex + CoQ10 |
| **Acid Reflux Drugs** | B12, calcium, iron | B12 injections + minerals |
| **Antibiotics** | Probiotics, B vitamins | Probiotics + B-Complex |
| **Thyroid Drugs** | Various minerals | Spacing protocol |

## Pro Tips

1. **Spacing matters** - Some supplements must be taken 2+ hours from medication
2. **Tell your doctor** - Don't hide supplement use; most interactions are manageable
3. **Get tested** - If on nutrient-depleting drugs, get blood work to assess deficiency
4. **Consistency is key** - Depletion support works best if consistent
5. **Medication changes** - When drugs change, supplement needs may change
