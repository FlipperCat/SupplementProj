## Best Collagen Supplements: Form Matters More Than Brand

Not all collagen is equal. Form (hydrolysis level), source, and bioavailability vary dramatically. This guide compares forms first, then top brands.

---

## Collagen Forms: Which Is Best?

| Form | Type | Absorption | Best For | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Peptides (Hydrolyzed)** | Broken down collagen | 90%+ | Skin, joints, hair | $$ |
| **Powder (Collagen)** | Whole collagen | 40-60% | Joint support | $ |
| **Capsules** | Encapsulated peptides | 85%+ | Convenience | $$$ |
| **Liquid** | Pre-dissolved | 95%+ | Maximum absorption | $$$$ |
| **Bone Broth** | Natural collagen source | 30-50% | Whole food | $$ |

**Most effective:** Hydrolyzed collagen peptides (best bioavailability + cost)

## Storage & Quality

**How to tell quality collagen:**
- **Mixes easily** (no lumps)
- **Neutral taste** (unflavored)
- **Dissolves completely**
- **No chemical smell**
- **Batch tested** (third-party verification)

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry place
- Sealed container
- Avoid heat/light
- Shelf life: 2-3 years if stored properly
