## Why the Extract Type Matters

Ashwagandha's benefits come from its active compounds — primarily withanolides, but also glycowithanolides, alkaloids, and other phytochemicals. Different extraction methods concentrate different profiles of these compounds, which is why:

- **KSM-66 and Sensoril produce predictable, clinically-validated results**
- **Generic extracts produce variable, unpredictable results**
- **Research on one extract does not transfer to another**

When a study shows "ashwagandha reduces cortisol by 30%," that result applies to the specific extract used in that study. Taking a random generic ashwagandha and expecting the same outcome is like buying a generic phone charger and expecting it to fast-charge.

## KSM-66: The Performance Extract

### Profile

KSM-66 is produced by Ixoreal Biomed using a proprietary alcohol-free extraction process. It is standardized to 1.5-2.5% withanolides and uses a full-spectrum root and leaf extract that preserves a broad range of active compounds.

### Where KSM-66 Excels

**Energy and vitality**: KSM-66's compound profile is energizing rather than sedating. Users consistently report improved daytime energy, motivation, and mental clarity without stimulant-like effects.

**Athletic performance**: This is KSM-66's standout advantage. Studies demonstrate:
- Increased strength and power output in resistance training
- Improved VO2 max and cardiorespiratory endurance
- Faster recovery between training sessions
- Healthy testosterone support in men

**Stress and anxiety**: KSM-66 reduces cortisol by approximately 26% and significantly lowers anxiety scores in multiple clinical trials. A landmark study showed 69% reduction in anxiety and insomnia scores.

**Sexual health**: Multiple studies show improvements in sexual function, fertility markers, and satisfaction in both men and women.

### Ideal User

- Wants an energizing adaptogen for daytime use
- Exercises regularly and wants performance benefits
- Deals with stress but also needs to stay sharp and focused
- Wants the most-studied extract with the broadest research base

### Dosing

- **Standard**: 300mg twice daily (morning and afternoon)
- **Performance**: 600mg once daily with breakfast
- **With food** for best absorption

## Sensoril: The Relaxation Extract

### Profile

Sensoril is produced by Natreon Inc. using root and leaf extraction standardized to 8-10% withanolides — approximately 4-5x the concentration of KSM-66. This higher concentration of glycowithanolides contributes to its distinct calming profile.

### Where Sensoril Excels

**Sleep quality**: Sensoril's standout benefit. Clinical studies show significant improvements in sleep onset, sleep quality, and overall restfulness. Its calming effect makes it an excellent addition to an evening routine.

**Cortisol reduction**: Sensoril achieves approximately 30% cortisol reduction — slightly higher than KSM-66. For those whose primary issue is elevated cortisol (chronic stress, belly fat, burnout), Sensoril has a modest edge.

**Anxiety and mood**: Equally effective as KSM-66 for anxiety reduction, with a more calming subjective experience. Users often describe feeling "deeply relaxed" rather than just "less stressed."

**Dose efficiency**: Because of its higher withanolide concentration, Sensoril is effective at lower doses (125-300mg vs 300-600mg). This means smaller capsules and fewer pills.

### Ideal User

- Primary goal is better sleep and nighttime relaxation
- Experiences significant anxiety or chronic cortisol elevation
- Prefers smaller doses and fewer capsules
- Does not need an energizing or performance-focused supplement
- Wants maximum withanolide delivery per milligram

### Dosing

- **Standard**: 125-250mg once daily in the evening
- **Sleep focus**: 250-300mg 30-60 minutes before bed
- **Can be taken without food** (effective either way)

## Generic Ashwagandha: The Budget Tier

### Profile

Generic ashwagandha extracts vary enormously in quality. They may be root-only, leaf-only, or whole-plant, with withanolide content ranging from less than 1% to occasionally 2-5% (if standardized). There is no patented process, no batch-to-batch consistency guarantee, and no specific clinical trial backing.

### What You Get

- Lower cost ($8-15 per month)
- Uncertain withanolide content unless independently tested
- Some general adaptogenic benefit is possible at higher doses
- No clinical studies on the specific extract you are taking

### When It Is Acceptable

- Your budget genuinely cannot accommodate $15-25/month
- You find a product explicitly standardized to at least 2.5% withanolides with third-party testing
- You use it as an introduction to ashwagandha before investing in a patented extract
- You understand results will be less predictable

### When to Avoid

- You have specific health goals (sleep, anxiety, performance) and want reliable outcomes
- The label does not specify withanolide percentage
- The price is suspiciously low (under $8/month)
- No third-party testing is mentioned
