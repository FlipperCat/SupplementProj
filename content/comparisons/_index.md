## Comparison Framework

When choosing between supplements, we compare:

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **Form/Type** | Directly affects absorption (bioavailability) |
| **Quality/Testing** | Ensures you're getting what's listed |
| **Cost Per Dose** | Sometimes premium costs less per serving |
| **Bioavailability** | 40% absorption ≠ 80% absorption |
| **User Results** | Real-world effectiveness |
| **Taste/Usability** | Will you actually take it consistently? |

## Brand Quality Tiers

### **Premium Brands** ($)
- Examples: Thorne, Pure Encapsulations, NSF-certified
- Bioavailability: 80%+
- Testing: Rigorous third-party
- Cost: Higher
- Best for: Serious health optimization

### **Mid-Range Brands** ($$)
- Examples: NOW Foods, Nutricost, Standard Process
- Bioavailability: 60-80%
- Testing: Moderate third-party
- Cost: Moderate
- Best for: Regular supplementation

### **Budget Brands** ($$$)
- Examples: Store brands, amazon basics
- Bioavailability: 40-60%
- Testing: Limited/none
- Cost: Lowest
- Best for: Trying new supplements, budget constraints

## Pro Shopping Tips

1. **Don't just buy cheapest** - Bioavailability difference makes premium worth it
2. **Cost per dose matters** - Higher price/unit ≠ higher total cost (higher absorption = less needed)
3. **Read reviews** - But remember: expensive ≠ effective
4. **Get testing info** - Third-party tested brands are trustworthy
5. **Try one month** - See how you feel before committing to 6-month supply
