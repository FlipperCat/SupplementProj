## Best Probiotics: Strain Selection Matters More Than Brand

Not all probiotics are equal. Strain selection determines effectiveness. This guide compares strains first, then brands.

---

## Probiotic Strains: Which Actually Work?

| Strain | Best For | Research | CFU Dose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lactobacillus plantarum** | IBS, immune, bloating | Strong | 10+ billion |
| **Bifidobacterium longum** | IBS, energy, mood | Strong | 10+ billion |
| **Lactobacillus helveticus** | Mood, anxiety, stress | Emerging | 10+ billion |
| **Saccharomyces boulardii** | Diarrhea, C. diff | Strong | 5+ billion |
| **Akkermansia muciniphila** | Leaky gut, immune | Emerging | 5+ billion |
| **Faecalibacterium prausnitzii** | Inflammation, IBD | Emerging | 5+ billion |
| **Lactobacillus rhamnosus** | Immune, IBS-D | Moderate | 10+ billion |
| **Lactobacillus acidophilus** | General health | Weak (oversold) | 10+ billion |

**Key insight:** Generic "acidophilus" strains are heavily marketed but have weak evidence. Specific strains matter.

## CFU Counts Explained

**Common misconception:** "Higher CFU = better"

**Reality:** Depends on goal
- **Maintenance:** 10-25 billion CFU daily
- **Mild issues:** 25-50 billion CFU daily
- **Serious issues (IBS, IBD):** 50-100+ billion CFU daily
- **Extreme:** 400+ billion (VSL#3 medical)

**Cost per billion CFU:**
- VSL#3: $0.18 per billion
- Renew Life: $0.20 per billion
- NOW Foods: $0.65 per billion
- Culturelle: $2.00 per billion

**Value:** More CFU ≠ better; proper strain selection more important
