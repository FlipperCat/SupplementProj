## Comparison Framework

### Quality Testing
- **ConsumerLab tested** (gold standard): Thorne, Pure Encapsulations, Nordic Naturals
- **NSF certified:** Nordic Naturals
- **Third-party tested:** NOW, Nutricost (less rigorous)
- **Non-tested:** Budget brands (avoid)

### EPA+DHA Content
- All major brands deliver 600-700 mg EPA+DHA per dose
- Difference is minimal (5-10% variation)
- More important: consistency across batches

### Cost Per Gram EPA+DHA
- **Thorne:** $3.21/gram (premium)
- **Pure Encapsulations:** $2.86/gram (premium)
- **Nordic Naturals:** $2.69/gram (excellent value)
- **NOW Foods:** $1.54/gram (good value)
- **Nutricost:** $1.17/gram (maximum value)

### Oxidation Stability
- **Excellent:** Thorne, Nordic Naturals, Pure Encapsulations
- **Good:** NOW Foods
- **Adequate:** Nutricost

(Oxidized fish oil loses potency and becomes rancid—quality matters here)

## Omega-3 Form Comparison

**Fish Oil (discussed above)**
- Most researched form
- Best EPA+DHA ratio
- Potential for fish burps
- Best absorption

**Algae-Based Omega-3** (vegetarian alternative)
- No fish burps
- Adequate EPA+DHA
- Higher cost
- Good for vegans/vegetarians

**Krill Oil** (premium alternative)
- Excellent absorption
- More expensive
- Unique phospholipid form
- Research emerging

## Storage & Stability

**Proper storage:**
- Cool, dark place
- Sealed container
- Avoid heat/light/oxygen

**Oxidation signs:**
- Rancid smell (fish smell too strong)
- Color change
- Cloudy softgels
- Thick/congealed liquid

**Best brands for stability:**
1. Thorne (best)
2. Nordic Naturals
3. Pure Encapsulations
4. NOW Foods
5. Nutricost
