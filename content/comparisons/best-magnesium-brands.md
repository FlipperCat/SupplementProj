## Magnesium Bioavailability Comparison

**Absorption Rate (% of dose absorbed):**
1. Magnesium Glycinate: 90%+ (best)
2. Magnesium Threonate: 90%+ (excellent, plus crosses BBB)
3. Magnesium Malate: 85%+ (very good)
4. Magnesium Citrate: 85%+ (very good)
5. Magnesium Bisglycinate: 90%+ (excellent)
6. Magnesium Oxide: 4% (AVOID - barely absorbed)

**Cost Per Absorption Unit:**
- Glycinate: Best value ($0.03-0.05 per % absorption)
- Threonate: Expensive but crosses BBB ($0.07 per % absorption)
- Citrate: Good value ($0.02 per % absorption)
- Malate: Good value ($0.03 per % absorption)
