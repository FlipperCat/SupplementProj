## How to Use These Timelines

### Step 1: Find Your Supplement
Select the supplement timeline that matches what you're taking.

### Step 2: Understand Realistic Expectations
Read what happens at each stage. Notice:
- When you should see results (don't judge too early)
- What real progress looks like
- When to adjust/switch if not working

### Step 3: Track Your Progress
Compare your actual results to the timeline:
- **Ahead of schedule?** Great! You're responding well
- **On schedule?** Perfect! Stay consistent
- **Behind schedule?** Check variables: consistency, dosage, lifestyle factors
- **No results by week 3-4?** May not work for you (individual variation)

### Step 4: Adjust If Needed
- **If working:** Continue consistently
- **If plateauing:** May need to cycle or adjust dosage
- **If not working:** Try different supplement or dosage

## Key Timeline Principles

### 1. Individual Variation is Real
Some people respond in days; others take weeks. Genetics, health status, and lifestyle all affect timelines.

### 2. Consistency Matters Most
- Taking daily = results
- Skipping days = delayed or no results
- You must commit to 4+ weeks before judging

### 3. Early Results Are Often Not "Real"
- Week 1 changes are usually placebo or water weight
- Real changes take 2-4 weeks
- Judge at week 3-4, not week 1

### 4. Supplement Timelines Assume:
- Proper dosage (check our guides)
- Daily consistency (no skipping)
- Complementary lifestyle (diet, exercise, sleep)
- 4+ weeks trial period

### 5. Plateau Effects Are Normal
- Most supplements plateau by month 2-3
- This is normal (body adaptation)
- Solutions: cycle supplements, adjust dosage, address lifestyle

## Common Timeline Mistakes

❌ **Judging at week 1**
- Too early; most supplements need 2-4 weeks
- Week 1 is mostly neural adaptation/placebo

❌ **Skipping doses**
- Kills results; must be daily
- Consistency is more important than brand

❌ **Wrong dosage**
- Taking too little = slow/no results
- Most people underdose

❌ **Not addressing lifestyle**
- Supplements enhance lifestyle; don't replace it
- Sleep, diet, exercise matter more

❌ **Expecting immediate results**
- Most supplements need 3-4 weeks
- Hair/skin timeline is even longer (months)

## Your Timeline Questions Answered

**Q: When should I see results?**
A: See the specific supplement timeline. Most show results by week 3-4. Hair/skin take longer (months).

**Q: What if I don't see results by week 2?**
A: Normal. Week 2 is too early for most supplements. Assess at week 3-4.

**Q: What if I don't see results by week 4?**
A: Check dosage (may be underdosed), consistency (skipping days?), and lifestyle. If still no results, may not work for you (individual variation).

**Q: Can I speed up results?**
A: Higher dosage, perfect consistency, and addressing lifestyle (sleep, diet, exercise) are the keys.

**Q: What if results plateau?**
A: Normal by month 2-3. Solution: cycle supplements (take break, restart), adjust dosage, or address lifestyle factors.
