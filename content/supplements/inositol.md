**What it is:** Myo-inositol is a sugar alcohol and phospholipid component that supports insulin signaling, metabolic health, and hormone balance. Research shows particular value for PCOS management, metabolic optimization, and mood regulation.

## Benefits

### Insulin Sensitivity & Metabolic Health
- Improves insulin signaling
- Enhances insulin sensitivity
- Supports healthy glucose metabolism
- May reduce PCOS symptoms (particularly valuable)
- Metabolic optimization

### Hormone Balance (PCOS)
- Particularly effective for PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome)
- May restore ovulatory cycles
- Reduces testosterone in PCOS
- Improves fertility in PCOS
- Reduces PCOS-related symptoms

### Mood & Cognitive Support
- May support mood regulation
- Reduces anxiety symptoms
- Supports emotional balance
- Mood stabilization effects
- Complements mental health strategies

### Cardiovascular Support
- May improve lipid profiles
- Supports cardiovascular health
- Reduces inflammation markers
- Blood pressure support (modest)

### Fertility Support
- Particularly valuable for women with PCOS
- May improve egg quality
- Supports ovulation
- Fertility enhancement

## Dosage

**Metabolic support:** 2,000 mg daily
**Enhanced:** 2,000-3,000 mg daily (split doses optimal)
**Full spectrum:** 3,000-4,000 mg daily
**Timing:** With meals
**Duration:** 4-8 weeks for initial effects; 12+ weeks for full metabolic benefits

## Best Forms

**Myo-Inositol Powder** (Recommended)
- Most cost-effective
- Easy to dose
- Mix in drinks
- Fast absorption

**Myo-Inositol Capsules**
- Convenient dosing
- More expensive per dose

## Research

Improves insulin sensitivity and glucose metabolism. PCOS symptom reduction documented. Fertility improvements in PCOS shown. Mood support demonstrated. Extensive research with excellent safety.

## Safety

Excellent safety profile. May cause mild GI upset at high doses (rare). Well-tolerated long-term.

## Stacking

**For PCOS:** Inositol 2-3g + Vitamin D3 2,000 IU + Chromium 200 mcg + Cinnamon 1-2g

**For metabolic health:** Inositol 2g + Chromium 200 mcg + Alpha Lipoic Acid 300 mg + Magnesium 300 mg

**For mood:** Inositol 2g + Magnesium 300 mg + Omega-3 2g + Vitamin D3 2,000 IU

## Bottom Line

Insulin-sensitizing nutrient supporting metabolic health, hormone balance, and mood regulation.
