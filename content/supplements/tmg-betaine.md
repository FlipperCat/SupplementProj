**What it is:** TMG (Trimethylglycine), also called betaine, is a methyl donor compound essential for methylation—a fundamental biochemical process underlying detoxification, energy production, and gene expression. TMG supplementation supports cardiovascular health via homocysteine reduction, cellular energy, and longevity pathways.

## What Is TMG/Betaine?

TMG is a naturally occurring amino acid derivative found in beets, spinach, whole grains, and seafood. It contains three methyl groups (-CH3) that it donates to metabolic processes, making it a critical methyl donor in the body.

TMG serves as:

- **Methyl donor:** Provides methyl groups for methylation reactions
- **Homocysteine reducer:** Converts homocysteine to methionine (lowering cardiovascular risk)
- **Detoxification support:** Methylation essential for Phase II detoxification
- **Energy production:** Supports mitochondrial ATP and creatine synthesis

## Benefits

### Cardiovascular Health & Homocysteine Reduction
TMG's primary benefit is cardiovascular support:

- Reduces homocysteine levels 15-25% (independent cardiovascular risk factor)
- Improves endothelial function
- Supports blood pressure regulation
- Reduces atherosclerotic risk markers
- Results visible in 4-8 weeks

### Detoxification Support
TMG enables Phase II methylation detoxification:

- Many toxins require methylation for elimination
- Enhanced liver detoxification capacity
- Supports hormonal balance (estrogen metabolism)
- Improved detoxification resilience

### Cellular Energy Production
TMG supports ATP synthesis and creatine production:

- Optimizes creatine synthesis (energy molecule)
- Reduces fatigue in methylation-deficient individuals
- Enhances exercise performance
- Supports muscle recovery

### Brain Health & Methylation
Adequate methylation essential for neurological function:

- Supports neurotransmitter synthesis (dopamine, serotonin, acetylcholine)
- Supports myelin formation (nerve insulation)
- Reduces neuroinflammation
- Supports cognitive function

## Dosage Recommendations

**Cardiovascular support:** 500-1,000 mg daily

**Enhanced detoxification/energy:** 1,000-1,500 mg daily

**Full spectrum:** 1,500-2,000 mg daily (typically 500-1,000 mg twice daily)

**Timing:** With meals

**Duration:** 4-8 weeks for cardiovascular benefits

**Note:** Effectiveness enhanced when B vitamins (B6, B12, folate) also adequate.
