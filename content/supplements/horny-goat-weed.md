**What it is:** Horny goat weed (Epimedium) is a traditional Chinese herbal supplement containing icariin—a compound that works similarly to pharmaceutical PDE5 inhibitors by enhancing blood flow and nitric oxide production. Used for centuries to support sexual performance, modern research validates its mechanisms for erectile enhancement.

## Benefits

### Sexual Performance & Erectile Function
- Improves erectile function and sexual arousal
- Enhances sexual satisfaction
- Increases sexual desire and libido
- Natural alternative to pharmaceutical options
- Works by improving blood flow

### Blood Flow & Circulation
- Enhances vasodilation and blood vessel widening
- Improves nitric oxide production
- Supports peripheral circulation
- Cardiovascular function support

### Energy & Stamina
- May improve exercise endurance
- Supports muscle energy and performance
- Enhances overall vitality
- Complements fitness efforts

## Dosage

**Erectile support:** 500-750 mg as-needed or daily
**Enhanced:** 750-1,000 mg daily or before activity
**Full:** 1,000-1,500 mg daily
**Timing:** 30-60 minutes before sexual activity or daily
**Duration:** 2-4 weeks to assess effects

## Best Forms

**Horny Goat Weed Extract** (Recommended)
- Standardized to icariin content
- More potent than whole herb
- Better absorption
- Consistent results

## Research

Improves erectile function comparable to some pharmaceutical alternatives. Icariin mechanism works similarly to PDE5 inhibitors. Improved blood flow documented. Sexual performance improvements shown.

## Safety

Generally safe with mild side effects (headache, flushing rare). No serious adverse events at therapeutic doses.

## Stacking

**For sexual performance:** Horny Goat Weed 750 mg + L-Arginine 3g + Ginseng 400 mg + Zinc 30 mg

**For blood flow:** Horny Goat Weed 750 mg + L-Citrulline 3g + Carnosine 1g + Maca 500 mg

Natural herbal supporting sexual performance through icariin compound and improved blood flow. Strong evidence for erectile enhancement.
