**What it is:** Ginseng (Panax ginseng/Asian ginseng) is a traditional adaptogenic herb containing ginsenosides that support energy, cognitive function, immune resilience, and stress adaptation. Used in traditional Chinese medicine for millennia, extensive modern research validates its multiple health benefits.

## Benefits

### Energy & Vitality
- Improves energy and reduces fatigue
- Supports sustained performance
- Enhances physical endurance
- May reduce exercise-induced fatigue
- Results visible within 1-2 weeks

### Stress Resilience & Adaptation
- Supports nervous system resilience to stress
- Reduces stress response markers
- Promotes emotional stability
- Enhances recovery from stress
- Overall stress tolerance improvement

### Cognitive Function
- Improves mental clarity and focus
- Supports memory and learning
- Reduces mental fatigue
- Enhances attention and concentration
- Neuroprotective effects

### Immune Function
- Supports natural killer cell activity
- Enhances immune response
- May reduce infection incidence
- Complements immune strategies

### Athletic Performance
- Improves endurance and power output
- Supports recovery post-exercise
- Reduces exercise-induced fatigue
- Athletic resilience enhancement

## Dosage

**Energy + stress:** 400-500 mg daily
**Enhanced support:** 500-600 mg daily
**Full spectrum:** 600-800 mg daily
**Timing:** Morning with breakfast
**Duration:** 2-4 weeks for initial effects; 8+ weeks for full adaptation

## Best Forms

**Panax Ginseng Extract** (Recommended)
- Standardized to ginsenosides
- Concentrated form
- Consistent results
- Superior bioavailability

## Research

Improves energy and cognitive function. Stress resilience and adaptation documented. Athletic performance enhancement shown. Extensive safety research with long history of use.

## Safety

Excellent safety profile. Very rare mild insomnia if taken too late. Generally well-tolerated long-term.

## Stacking

**For energy + cognition:** Ginseng 600 mg + Rhodiola 400 mg + L-Theanine 200 mg + B-Complex

**For stress resilience:** Ginseng 600 mg + Ashwagandha 300 mg + Schisandra 750 mg + Magnesium 300 mg

**For athletic:** Ginseng 600 mg + Rhodiola 400 mg + Carnosine 1g + Creatine 5g

## Bottom Line

Adaptogenic herb that improves energy, cognitive function, and stress resilience through multiple mechanisms.
