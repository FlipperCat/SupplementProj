**What it is:** Pomegranate extract from Punica granatum fruit is rich in punicalagins and anthocyanins that support cardiovascular health, reduce inflammation, and provide potent antioxidant protection.

## Benefits

### Cardiovascular Health
- Reduces blood pressure (5-8 mmHg typical)
- Improves endothelial function and blood flow
- Reduces arterial plaque formation
- Supports cholesterol profiles
- Anti-inflammatory in cardiovascular system

### Antioxidant Protection
- Punicalagins among most potent antioxidants known
- Protects against LDL oxidation
- Protects cellular DNA
- Supports mitochondrial health
- Anti-aging antioxidant support

### Anti-Inflammatory
- Reduces TNF-alpha and IL-6
- Systemic inflammation reduction
- Supports joint health

### Sexual Function Support
- Improves blood flow to tissues
- May improve erectile function
- Improved nitric oxide and endothelial function

## Dosage

**Cardiovascular:** 250-500 mg daily
**Enhanced:** 500-750 mg daily
**Full:** 750-1,000 mg daily
**Timing:** With meals containing fat
**Duration:** 4-8 weeks cardiovascular; 8+ weeks antioxidant benefits

## Best Forms

**Pomegranate Extract** (Recommended)
- Most concentrated
- Superior to juice
- Better absorption

**Pomegranate Juice**
- Whole fruit benefits
- Higher sugar content

## Research

Shows 5-8 mmHg BP reduction, improved endothelial function, potent antioxidants. Excellent safety record.

## Stacking

**Cardiovascular:** Pomegranate 500 mg + L-Arginine 3g + Omega-3 2g + Hawthorn 600 mg

**Antioxidant:** Pomegranate 500 mg + Resveratrol 500 mg + Vitamin C 500 mg + Vitamin E 400 IU

## Bottom Line

One of the most potent natural antioxidants with strong cardiovascular evidence.
