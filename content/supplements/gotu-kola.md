**What it is:** Gotu kola (Centella asiatica) is a traditional herb used in Ayurvedic and traditional Chinese medicine to support cognitive function, circulation, and skin health. It contains triterpenoids that support vascular function, mental clarity, and connective tissue health.

## Benefits

### Cognitive Function & Mental Clarity
- Improves memory and learning
- Supports mental clarity and focus
- Reduces mental fatigue
- Supports cognitive aging
- Neuroprotective effects

### Blood Flow & Circulation
- Enhances venous circulation
- Supports capillary health
- Improves peripheral blood flow
- Reduces vascular stasis
- Supports lymphatic function

### Skin Health & Connective Tissue
- Supports collagen synthesis
- Improves skin elasticity and texture
- Supports wound healing
- Supports joint and connective tissue health

### Anti-Inflammatory & Antioxidant
- Reduces systemic inflammation
- Provides antioxidant protection
- Supports tissue repair
- Complements healthy aging

## Dosage

**Cognitive support:** 300-500 mg daily
**Enhanced:** 500-750 mg daily
**Full:** 750-1,000 mg daily
**Timing:** With meals
**Duration:** 4-8 weeks for cognitive effects; 8+ weeks for circulation benefits

## Best Forms

**Gotu Kola Extract** (Recommended)
- Standardized, concentrated form
- Better bioavailability
- More convenient dosing
- Consistent results

## Research

Supports memory and cognitive function. Improves venous circulation and vascular health. Skin elasticity improvements documented. Safe and well-tolerated.

## Safety

Generally safe. Very rare mild headache in sensitive individuals. Well-tolerated long-term.

## Stacking

**For cognition:** Gotu Kola 500 mg + Bacopa 300 mg + Ginkgo 200 mg + L-Theanine 200 mg

**For circulation:** Gotu Kola 600 mg + Ginkgo 240 mg + L-Arginine 2g + Carnosine 1g

**For skin + collagen:** Gotu Kola 500 mg + Vitamin C 500 mg + Collagen peptides 10g + Hyaluronic acid 100 mg

Traditional herb supporting cognitive function, circulation, and skin health through multiple mechanisms.
