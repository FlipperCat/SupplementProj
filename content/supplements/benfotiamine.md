**What it is:** Benfotiamine is a fat-soluble form of vitamin B1 that crosses cell membranes more effectively than standard thiamine. It supports nerve health, metabolic function, and protects against glycation.

## Benefits

### Nerve Health & Neuroprotection
- Supports peripheral nerve function
- May help with neuropathic pain
- Supports myelin formation
- Protects nerves from oxidative stress
- Anti-inflammatory in nerve tissue

### Anti-Glycation Support
- Prevents advanced glycation end products
- Reduces protein damage from glucose
- Supports vascular health
- Complements blood sugar management

### Metabolic Support
- Essential cofactor for energy metabolism
- Supports ATP production in mitochondria
- Enhances carbohydrate metabolism
- Supports healthy glucose response

### Antioxidant Protection
- Reduces oxidative stress
- Protects cellular proteins and lipids
- Supports mitochondrial function

## Dosage

**Nerve health:** 100-150 mg daily
**Enhanced:** 150-200 mg daily
**Full:** 200-300 mg daily
**Timing:** With meals containing fat
**Duration:** 4-8 weeks initial; 12+ weeks for nerve benefits

## Best Forms

**Benfotiamine** (Recommended)
- Fat-soluble form
- Crosses cell membranes effectively
- Better bioavailability
- More potent for neuroprotection

**Standard Thiamine**
- Water-soluble, lower bioavailability
- Less effective for neuroprotection

## Research

Supports nerve health and neuropathic symptoms. Prevents glycation and protein damage. Metabolic support documented. Safe and well-tolerated.

## Safety

Excellent safety profile. B vitamins excreted; excess eliminated.

## Stacking

**For neuroprotection:** Benfotiamine 200 mg + Alpha Lipoic Acid 300 mg + Vitamin B12 1,000 mcg + Folate 400 mcg

**For metabolic:** Benfotiamine 200 mg + Chromium 200 mcg + Vitamin B6 25 mg + Magnesium 300 mg

## Bottom Line

Superior fat-soluble B1 supporting nerve health, anti-glycation, and metabolic function.

**Key takeaways:**
- Fat-soluble B1 for superior bioavailability
- Dose: 100-300 mg daily with meals
- Results in 4-8 weeks; nerve benefits 12+ weeks
- Excellent safety
- Synergizes with alpha lipoic acid and B vitamins
- Valuable for neuroprotection and metabolic health
