**What it is:** Maca is a Peruvian root vegetable nutrient-dense superfood containing amino acids, minerals, and compounds that support sexual function, fertility, and hormonal balance. Used traditionally for centuries, modern research validates its benefits for sexual performance and reproductive health.

## Benefits

### Sexual Function & Performance
- Improves sexual desire and arousal
- Supports erectile function and hardness
- Enhances sexual satisfaction
- Supports ejaculatory function
- Improves female sexual function too

### Fertility Support
- May improve semen quality and motility
- Supports reproductive health
- Complements fertility optimization
- May improve sperm count
- Particularly valuable for male fertility

### Hormonal Balance
- Supports healthy hormone levels
- May improve mood and emotional balance
- Energy and stamina support
- Overall vitality enhancement

### Nutrient Density
- Rich in amino acids and minerals
- Contains vitamins and bioactive compounds
- Whole food nutrient matrix
- Complements healthy diet

### Energy & Stamina
- May improve energy and endurance
- Supports physical performance
- Athletic resilience support
- Complements training

## Dosage

**Sexual function support:** 1,500-2,000 mg daily
**Enhanced fertility:** 2,000-2,500 mg daily
**Full spectrum:** 2,500-3,000 mg daily
**Timing:** With meals
**Duration:** 2-4 weeks for initial effects; 8+ weeks for full benefits

## Best Forms

**Maca Powder** (Recommended)
- Nutrient-dense whole food
- Most economical
- Mix in drinks or food
- Highest nutrient content

**Maca Capsules**
- Convenient dosing
- More expensive per dose

## Research

Improves sexual function in both men and women. Fertility and sperm quality improvements documented. Hormonal balance support shown. Well-tolerated with long history of use.

## Safety

Excellent safety profile. May cause mild GI upset if taken without food (rare). Well-tolerated long-term.

## Stacking

**For sexual performance:** Maca 2g + Horny Goat Weed 750 mg + L-Arginine 3g + Zinc 30 mg

**For fertility:** Maca 2g + Zinc 30 mg + Selenium 200 mcg + Carnitine 2g

**For hormonal:** Maca 2g + Tribulus 1g + Tongkat Ali 500 mg + Vitamin D3 2,000 IU

Nutrient-dense Peruvian root supporting sexual function, fertility, and hormonal balance through whole food nutrition.
