**What it is:** Taurine is a semi-essential amino acid synthesized from methionine and cysteine, abundant in the heart, brain, and muscles. It supports cardiac function, exercise performance, neurological health, and cellular energy production—making it valuable for cardiovascular optimization and athletic performance.

## Benefits

### Cardiac Function & Heart Health
- Strengthens cardiac contractility (force of heart contraction)
- Supports healthy heart rhythm
- Supports cardiovascular resilience
- May improve exercise tolerance
- Reduces cardiovascular disease markers

### Athletic Performance & Recovery
- Improves endurance and power output
- Reduces exercise-induced fatigue
- Accelerates recovery post-exercise
- Supports muscle performance
- Particularly effective for high-intensity exercise

### Neurological Support
- Supports brain cell function
- Protective against neurological stress
- Supports neurological aging
- Neuroprotective mechanisms
- Cognitive function support

### Eye Health
- Concentrated in retina
- Supports visual function
- Protective against vision aging
- Essential for retinal health

### Metabolic & Energy Support
- Supports mitochondrial function
- Enhances ATP production
- Supports cellular energy metabolism
- Metabolic optimization

## Dosage

**Cardiac support:** 1,000-1,500 mg daily
**Athletic focus:** 1,500-2,000 mg daily
**Full spectrum:** 2,000-3,000 mg daily
**Timing:** With meals (especially post-workout)
**Duration:** 2-4 weeks for initial effects; 8+ weeks for full benefits

## Best Forms

**Taurine Powder** (Recommended)
- Most cost-effective
- Easy to dose
- Mix in drinks or water
- Fast absorption

**Taurine Capsules**
- Convenient dosing
- More expensive per dose

## Research

Improves cardiac contractility and function. Enhanced athletic performance documented. Neurological protection shown. Excellent safety profile with extensive research.

## Safety

Excellent safety profile. No serious adverse events at therapeutic doses. Well-tolerated long-term.

## Stacking

**For cardiac health:** Taurine 2g + CoQ10 200 mg + Hawthorn 600 mg + Magnesium 300 mg

**For athletic:** Taurine 2g + Carnitine 1g + Carnosine 1g + Creatine 5g

**For neurological:** Taurine 2g + Lion's Mane 1g + Omega-3 2g + Vitamin D3 2,000 IU

## Bottom Line

Cardiac and neurological amino acid supporting heart health, athletic performance, and cellular function.
