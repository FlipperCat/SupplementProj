**What it is:** NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide) is a precursor to NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide)—a critical coenzyme for cellular energy, DNA repair, and longevity signaling. NMN supplementation restores NAD+ levels that decline with age, activating sirtuins and supporting cellular rejuvenation and aging optimization.

## Benefits

### Cellular Energy & Mitochondrial Function
- Restores NAD+ levels (decline ~50% by age 80)
- Enhances ATP production in mitochondria
- Improves mitochondrial function
- Supports cellular energy metabolism
- Reduces fatigue and improves vitality

### Longevity & Anti-Aging Pathways
- Activates sirtuins (longevity genes)
- Supports cellular rejuvenation
- Activates autophagy and cellular cleanup
- Extends lifespan in animal models 10-30%
- Reverses aging markers in research

### NAD+-Dependent DNA Repair
- NAD+ essential for DNA repair (PARPs)
- Supports genomic stability
- Protects against DNA damage
- Reduces cancer risk (theoretical)
- Supports healthy aging

### Cardiovascular & Metabolic Health
- Supports endothelial function
- Improves vascular health
- Supports metabolic health
- May improve insulin sensitivity
- Blood pressure regulation support

### Cognitive Function & Neuroprotection
- Crosses blood-brain barrier
- Supports neuronal mitochondria
- Neuroprotective effects
- Supports cognitive aging
- May reduce neurodegeneration risk

## Dosage

**General longevity:** 250-500 mg daily
**Enhanced anti-aging:** 500-750 mg daily
**Full spectrum:** 750-1,000 mg daily
**Timing:** Morning with breakfast
**Duration:** Minimum 12 weeks before assessing; benefits build over time

## Best Forms

**NMN Capsules** (Recommended)
- Pre-dosed, convenient
- Stable storage
- Reliable absorption

## Research

Activates longevity pathways in research. Restores NAD+ levels. Extends lifespan 10-30% in animal models. Limited but promising human data. Sirtuins and autophagy activation documented.

## Safety

Excellent safety profile. Very limited human long-term data but extensive short-term safety data. No serious adverse events at therapeutic doses.

NAD+ precursor activating longevity pathways and supporting cellular energy. Strong animal evidence, emerging human validation.
