**What it is:** Bacopa monnieri is a traditional Ayurvedic herb used for millennia to support memory, learning, and cognitive function. It contains bacosides that protect neurons and enhance synaptic communication, making it one of the most well-researched cognitive herbs.

## Benefits

### Memory & Learning Support
- Improves memory formation and retention
- Enhances learning capacity
- Supports working memory
- Accelerates information processing
- Reduces mental fatigue

### Cognitive Function
- Improves mental clarity and focus
- Supports attention and concentration
- Reduces brain fog
- Enhances information retrieval
- Cognitive efficiency improvement

### Neuroprotection & Brain Aging
- Protects neurons from oxidative stress
- Supports neuronal synaptic communication
- May reduce cognitive decline with aging
- Antioxidant and anti-inflammatory
- Supports healthy brain aging

### Anxiety & Mood Support
- May reduce anxiety and stress
- Supports emotional balance
- Promotes relaxation without drowsiness
- Mood stabilization effects

### Antioxidant Protection
- Potent antioxidant activity
- Protects brain cells from damage
- Reduces oxidative stress
- Supports mitochondrial health

## Dosage

**Cognitive support:** 300-400 mg daily
**Enhanced memory:** 400-500 mg daily
**Full spectrum:** 500-600 mg daily
**Timing:** With meals containing fat (enhances absorption)
**Duration:** 4-8 weeks for initial effects; 12+ weeks for full cognitive benefits

## Best Forms

**Bacopa Extract** (Recommended)
- Standardized to bacosides
- Concentrated form
- Better bioavailability
- Most research uses extract

## Research

Improves memory and learning capacity. Cognitive function enhancement documented. Neuroprotection and brain aging support shown. Extensive research with excellent safety record.

## Safety

Excellent safety profile. May cause mild GI upset (rare). Well-tolerated long-term.

## Stacking

**For memory:** Bacopa 400 mg + Ginkgo 180 mg + Choline 550 mg + Vitamin B12 1,000 mcg

**For cognitive aging:** Bacopa 400 mg + Gotu Kola 500 mg + Lion's Mane 1g + Omega-3 2g

**For learning:** Bacopa 400 mg + Alpha-GPC 600 mg + L-Theanine 200 mg + Caffeine 200 mg

## Bottom Line

Traditional brain herb supporting memory, learning, and cognitive aging through neuroprotective mechanisms.
