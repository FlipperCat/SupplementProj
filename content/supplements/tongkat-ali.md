**What it is:** Tongkat Ali is a traditional Malaysian herbal containing quassinoids and other compounds that support testosterone production, sexual function, and athletic performance. Modern research validates its testosterone-boosting properties and muscle-building support.

## Benefits

### Testosterone Support
- May significantly support healthy testosterone levels
- Particularly effective when baseline is low
- Supports male vitality and confidence
- Athletic performance improvement
- Results documented in clinical research

### Sexual Function & Libido
- Improves sexual desire and arousal
- Supports erectile function
- Enhances sexual satisfaction
- Improves sexual performance
- Female sexual function support too

### Athletic Performance
- May improve muscle gains from training
- Supports strength and power output
- Enhances workout performance
- Faster recovery post-exercise
- Particularly valuable for athletes

### Overall Vitality & Energy
- May improve energy and mood
- Supports confidence and motivation
- Complements healthy lifestyle
- Athletic resilience support

## Dosage

**Testosterone support:** 300-400 mg daily
**Enhanced:** 400-600 mg daily
**Full spectrum:** 600-1,000 mg daily
**Timing:** With meals
**Duration:** 2-4 weeks for initial effects; 8+ weeks for full testosterone optimization

## Best Forms

**Tongkat Ali Extract** (Recommended)
- Standardized to eurycomanone content
- Concentrated, potent form
- Consistent results
- Better bioavailability

## Research

Supports healthy testosterone levels and sexual function. Athletic performance improvements documented. Muscle gain support shown. Clinical research validates effectiveness.

## Safety

Excellent safety profile. No serious adverse events at therapeutic doses. Well-tolerated long-term.
