**What it is:** Ginkgo biloba is an ancient tree extract used in traditional medicine to support blood flow, memory, and cognitive aging. It contains ginkgo flavones and terpenoids that improve cerebral and peripheral circulation while providing antioxidant neuroprotection.

## Benefits

### Cognitive Function & Memory
- Improves memory and learning
- Supports mental clarity and focus
- Reduces age-related cognitive decline
- Enhances information processing
- Neuroprotective effects

### Circulation & Blood Flow
- Improves cerebral blood flow
- Supports peripheral circulation
- Enhances oxygen delivery to tissues
- Supports vascular function
- May improve exercise performance

### Aging Brain Support
- Particularly valuable for cognitive aging
- Reduces neuroinflammation
- Protects neurons from oxidative stress
- May reduce dementia risk
- Supports healthy aging

### Antioxidant & Neuroprotection
- Potent antioxidant activity
- Protects brain cells from damage
- Reduces oxidative stress
- Complements healthy aging

## Dosage

**Cognitive support:** 120-180 mg daily
**Enhanced:** 180-240 mg daily (often 60 mg three times daily)
**Timing:** With meals
**Duration:** 4-8 weeks for initial effects; 12+ weeks for full cognitive benefits

## Best Forms

**Ginkgo Extract** (Recommended)
- Standardized to flavones and terpenoids
- Concentrated, effective form
- Consistent results
- Most research uses extract

## Research

Improves memory and cognitive function. Supports blood flow and cerebral oxygenation. Reduces age-related cognitive decline. Safe with long history of use.

## Safety

Excellent safety profile. May have mild blood-thinning effects. Well-tolerated long-term.

## Stacking

**For cognitive aging:** Ginkgo 180 mg + Gotu Kola 500 mg + Bacopa 300 mg + Lion's Mane 1g

**For circulation:** Ginkgo 180 mg + Hawthorn 600 mg + L-Arginine 2g + Carnosine 1g

**For memory:** Ginkgo 180 mg + Bacopa 300 mg + Vitamin B12 1,000 mcg + Choline 550 mg

## Bottom Line

Ancient tree extract supporting memory, blood flow, and cognitive aging through proven mechanisms.
