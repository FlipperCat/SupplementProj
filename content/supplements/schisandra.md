**What it is:** Schisandra is a traditional adaptogenic berry used in traditional Chinese medicine to build resilience, support mental clarity, and enhance physical endurance. It contains lignans that support stress adaptation, cognitive function, and physical performance.

## Benefits

### Stress Resilience & Adaptation
- Supports nervous system resilience
- Reduces stress response
- Promotes emotional balance
- Supports stress recovery
- Enhances stress tolerance

### Mental Clarity & Cognitive Function
- Improves focus and concentration
- Reduces mental fatigue
- Supports memory and learning
- Enhanced mental clarity
- Cognitive resilience

### Physical Endurance & Performance
- Improves exercise tolerance
- Supports recovery post-exercise
- May increase time-to-fatigue
- Enhances athletic performance
- Supports training capacity

### Liver Support & Detoxification
- Traditional liver support
- Supports detoxification capacity
- Promotes healthy liver function
- Complements comprehensive detox strategies

### Sleep Quality
- May support restful sleep
- Calming effects at appropriate doses
- Supports sleep quality and recovery
- Particularly helpful evening dosing

## Dosage

**Stress support:** 500-750 mg daily
**Enhanced:** 750-1,000 mg daily
**Full:** 1,000-1,500 mg daily
**Timing:** With meals
**Duration:** 2-4 weeks for initial effects; 8+ weeks for full adaptation

## Best Forms

**Schisandra Extract** (Recommended)
- Concentrated, standardized form
- Better absorption
- More convenient dosing

## Research

Supports stress resilience and adaptation. Mental clarity and cognitive improvements documented. Physical endurance enhancement shown. Sleep quality support demonstrated.

## Safety

Generally safe. Very rare mild insomnia if taken too late in day. Well-tolerated long-term.

## Stacking

**For stress + cognition:** Schisandra 1g + Rhodiola 500 mg + Ginseng 400 mg + L-Theanine 200 mg

**For athletic:** Schisandra 1g + Rhodiola 500 mg + Carnosine 1g + Creatine 5g

**For sleep:** Schisandra 500 mg + Reishi 1g + L-Theanine 200 mg + Magnesium 300 mg

## Bottom Line

Adaptogenic berry supporting stress resilience, mental clarity, and physical endurance through multiple mechanisms.
