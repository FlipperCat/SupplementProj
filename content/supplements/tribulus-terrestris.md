**What it is:** Tribulus terrestris is a traditional herbal containing saponin compounds that support testosterone production, sexual function, and athletic performance. Used in Ayurvedic and traditional medicine for millennia, modern research validates its testosterone and libido-supporting properties.

## Benefits

### Testosterone Support
- May support healthy testosterone levels
- Particularly effective when baseline is low
- Supports sexual function through hormonal mechanism
- Athletic performance improvement

### Sexual Function & Libido
- Improves sexual desire and arousal
- Supports erectile function
- Enhances sexual satisfaction
- Works for both men and women

### Athletic Performance
- May improve muscle gains from training
- Supports strength and power output
- Enhances workout performance
- Recovery support

### Overall Vitality
- May improve energy and mood
- Supports general well-being
- Complements healthy lifestyle

## Dosage

**Sexual support:** 500-750 mg daily
**Enhanced:** 750-1,000 mg daily
**Full:** 1,000-1,500 mg daily
**Timing:** With meals
**Duration:** 4-8 weeks to assess effects

## Best Forms

**Tribulus Extract** (Recommended)
- Standardized to saponin content
- More potent than whole herb
- Consistent results
- Better absorption

## Research

Supports healthy testosterone levels and sexual function. Athletic performance improvements documented. Libido enhancement shown. Safe and well-tolerated.

## Safety

Generally safe with no serious adverse events at therapeutic doses. Well-tolerated long-term.
