**What it is:** Rhodiola rosea is a traditional adaptogenic herb containing rosavins and salidroside that support stress resilience, reduce fatigue, and enhance cognitive and physical performance. Used for decades in Russian and Scandinavian medicine, modern research validates its fatigue-reducing and stress-supporting properties.

## Benefits

### Fatigue Reduction & Energy
- Reduces mental and physical fatigue
- Improves energy and vitality
- Supports sustained performance
- Particularly effective for chronic stress-induced fatigue
- Results visible within 1-2 weeks

### Stress Resilience & Mood
- Supports nervous system resilience to stress
- May improve mood and outlook
- Reduces stress response markers
- Promotes emotional stability
- Enhances stress recovery

### Cognitive Function
- Improves mental clarity and focus
- Supports attention and concentration
- Reduces mental fatigue
- Supports memory and learning
- Neuroprotective effects

### Athletic Performance & Recovery
- May improve endurance and power output
- Supports faster recovery post-exercise
- Enhances exercise tolerance
- Reduces exercise-induced fatigue
- Complements training

## Dosage

**Fatigue + stress:** 200-300 mg daily
**Enhanced support:** 300-400 mg daily
**Full spectrum:** 400-600 mg daily
**Timing:** Morning with breakfast
**Duration:** 2-4 weeks for initial effects; 8+ weeks for full adaptation

## Best Forms

**Rhodiola Extract** (Recommended)
- Standardized to rosavins and salidroside
- Concentrated, potent form
- Consistent results
- Better bioavailability

## Research

Reduces fatigue and improves cognitive performance. Stress resilience improvements documented. Athletic performance enhancement shown. Excellent safety record with extensive use history.

## Safety

Excellent safety profile. Very rare mild insomnia if taken too late. Generally well-tolerated long-term.
