## Our Mission

Supplement Guide exists to provide clear, research-backed information about supplements, vitamins, and peptides — helping you make informed decisions about your health.

The supplement industry is full of hype, misinformation, and misleading claims. We cut through the noise to deliver:

- **Evidence-based information** grounded in peer-reviewed research
- **Practical recommendations** you can actually use
- **Honest assessments** of what works and what doesn't
- **Clear explanations** of how supplements interact with each other and medications

## What We Cover

### Individual Supplements
Comprehensive guides to specific supplements including:
- What it does and how it works
- Dosage recommendations
- Best forms and timing
- When to expect results
- Side effects and interactions

### Stacks & Combinations
Understanding how supplements work together:
- Synergistic combinations
- What NOT to take together
- Optimal timing and dosing

### Medication Guides
Supplements to support common medications:
- What nutrients medications deplete
- Supplements that enhance effectiveness
- Critical interactions to avoid

### Goal-Based Guides
Recommendations organized by what you want to achieve:
- Better sleep
- More energy
- Improved focus
- Stress relief
- Athletic performance

## Our Approach

### Research-First
We prioritize peer-reviewed studies, meta-analyses, and clinical trials. When evidence is limited, we say so.

### Practical Application
Theory is useless without application. We provide specific dosages, timing recommendations, and real-world protocols.

### No Conflicts of Interest
While we use affiliate links to support the site, our recommendations are based on evidence and quality — not commission rates. We recommend products we'd take ourselves.

### Transparency
We're clear about:
- The strength of evidence for each claim
- When something is emerging research vs. well-established
- Our use of affiliate links
- The limitations of supplement science

## Who We're For

- People who want to optimize their health with supplements
- Those taking medications who want to understand interactions
- Health-conscious individuals seeking reliable information
- Anyone tired of supplement industry hype

## Medical Disclaimer

The information on this site is for educational purposes only and is not intended as medical advice. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider before starting any supplement regimen, especially if you take medications or have health conditions.

We are not doctors. We're researchers and enthusiasts committed to sharing high-quality information.
