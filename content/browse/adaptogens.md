intro: |
  Adaptogens are natural substances that help your body resist and adapt to physical, chemical, and biological stressors. They work by modulating the HPA axis (your stress response system) to bring cortisol and other stress hormones into balance.

  Unlike stimulants that push you in one direction, adaptogens **normalize** — calming you when stressed, energizing you when fatigued. Most need 2-4 weeks of consistent use to show full effects.
