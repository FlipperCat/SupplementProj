  Vitamins are essential organic compounds your body needs in small amounts for proper function. They're divided into **fat-soluble** (A, D, E, K — stored in body fat, taken with meals containing fat) and **water-soluble** (B vitamins, C — not stored, need regular intake).

  Most people are deficient in at least one vitamin. Vitamin D deficiency alone affects an estimated 42% of adults. Below are our complete guides to every vitamin supplement.
