## How To Choose

1. **Find your primary goal** above
2. **Read the complete protocol** with:
   - Essential stack (lower budget)
   - Optimal stack (best results)
   - Premium stack (comprehensive support)
3. **Check realistic expectations** - when you'll see results
4. **Review monthly costs** - budget $50-200 depending on stack
5. **Check medication interactions** if applicable

## Pro Tips

- **Start with essentials** - Add more supplements only if fundamentals aren't working
- **Give it time** - Most supplements need 4-8 weeks to show results; some take 12 weeks
- **Combine strategically** - Some supplements synergize (e.g., D3 with calcium)
- **Food first** - Supplements optimize nutrition, not replace it
- **Check your meds** - Some supplements interact with prescriptions
