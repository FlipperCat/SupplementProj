## Stress & Cortisol: Supplement Strategy

Chronic stress elevates cortisol, disrupts sleep, damages immunity, and accelerates aging. Supplements support:
- Cortisol normalization
- Nervous system balance (parasympathetic activation)
- Stress resilience and adaptation
- Anxiety reduction
- Sleep and recovery optimization

**Note:** Supplements support lifestyle changes. Stress management requires behavioral intervention.

---

## Tier 1: Adaptogenic Herbs (Stress Resilience)

### Rhodiola (Stress Resilience)

**Why essential:**
- Adapts body to stress
- Reduces cortisol and stress perception
- Improves energy without stimulation
- Reduces anxiety
- Supports mental performance under stress

**Dose:** 300-600 mg daily (standardized extract)

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks for noticeable effect

**Cost:** $15-25/month

---

### Ashwagandha (Cortisol Control)

**Why critical:**
- Directly reduces cortisol levels
- Reduces anxiety and stress perception
- Improves sleep quality
- Supports immune function
- Well-researched for stress

**Dose:** 300-500 mg daily (standardized to withanolides)

**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks for cortisol reduction

**Cost:** $15-25/month

---

### Holy Basil (Tulsi) (Calm & Focus)

**Why beneficial:**
- Adapts to stress
- Reduces anxiety
- Improves mental clarity
- Supports sleep
- Gentle adaptogen (can take year-round)

**Dose:** 300-600 mg daily (or tea)

**Timeline:** 1-3 weeks for effect

**Cost:** $12-20/month

---

## Tier 2: Nervous System Support

### L-Theanine (Calm Alertness)

**Why essential:**
- Activates parasympathetic nervous system
- Reduces anxiety without drowsiness
- Improves focus with calm
- Works synergistically with adaptogens
- Safe and well-researched

**Dose:** 100-200 mg daily (or 2-3x for acute stress)

**Timeline:** 30-60 minutes for acute effect; chronic benefit builds

**Cost:** $10-18/month

---

### Magnesium (Stress & Muscle Relaxation)

**Why critical:**
- Depleted by chronic stress
- Supports nervous system relaxation
- Reduces muscle tension
- Improves sleep (critical for stress recovery)
- Often deficient during stress

**Dose:** 300-500 mg glycinate daily

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for muscle relaxation; sleep improvement faster

**Cost:** $8-12/month

---

## Tier 3: Sleep & Recovery Support

### Melatonin (Sleep Restoration)

**Why important:**
- Sleep disrupted by stress/cortisol
- Melatonin supports sleep
- Circadian rhythm restoration
- Antioxidant support
- Critical for stress recovery

**Dose:** 0.5-5 mg at bedtime

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for sleep improvement

**Cost:** $5-10/month

---

### Valerian Root (Deep Sleep)

**Why beneficial:**
- Traditional sleep herb
- Promotes deep sleep
- Reduces anxiety
- Works synergistically with magnesium
- Safe and well-tolerated

**Dose:** 300-600 mg at bedtime

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for sleep improvement

**Cost:** $10-15/month

---

## Tier 4: Anti-inflammatory & Recovery

### Omega-3 (Inflammation & Mood)

**Why important:**
- Chronic stress = inflammation
- Omega-3 reduces inflammation
- Supports mood and anxiety
- Brain protective during stress
- Sleep quality support

**Dose:** 2-3 grams EPA+DHA daily

**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks for inflammation reduction

**Cost:** $12-20/month
