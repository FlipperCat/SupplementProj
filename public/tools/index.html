Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools free?

Yes! All our tools are completely free to use. We're supported by affiliate commissions, so you're not paying anything extra.

Are the recommendations personalized?

Absolutely. Each tool uses YOUR inputs (goals, budget, health conditions, medications) to create personalized recommendations.

Should I consult my doctor?

Yes, especially if you're on medications or have health conditions. Our tools are educational and complement medical advice—they don't replace it.

Can I use multiple tools?

Yes! Many people start with the Quiz, then use the Comparison Tool to find brands, and the Medication Checker if they're on prescriptions.

How often should I update my stack?

Most stacks remain stable for 3-6 months. If your goals change or you experience side effects, regenerate your stack or consult the guides.

Where can I buy the recommended supplements?

We partner with Nutrilite/Amway and provide affiliate links. You can also purchase from other brands—all that matters is quality and potency.
