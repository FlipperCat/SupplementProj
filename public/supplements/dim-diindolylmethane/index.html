DIM (Diindolylmethane)

The broccoli-derived compound that supports healthy estrogen metabolism and hormonal balance

Research-Backed

Quick Facts

Typical Dosage 100-300 mg
Best Time With meals containing fat
Best Form Capsules
Results Timeline 4-8 weeks

What it is: DIM is a phytonutrient derived from cruciferous vegetables (especially broccoli) that supports healthy estrogen metabolism and hormonal balance. By promoting favorable estrogen metabolites, DIM supports hormonal optimization for both men and women.

Benefits

Estrogen Metabolism & Hormonal Balance

  • Supports conversion of estrogen to favorable metabolites
  • Reduces accumulation of problematic estrogen metabolites
  • Promotes healthy estrogen-to-progesterone balance
  • Supports hormonal resilience
  • Particularly valuable for women during cycling years

Detoxification Support

  • Supports Phase II liver detoxification of estrogens
  • Works with glutathione pathway
  • Supports overall detoxification capacity
  • Complements comprehensive detox strategies

Women’s Health

  • May reduce hormonal symptoms and discomfort
  • Supports mood and emotional balance
  • May improve skin clarity
  • Supports energy and vitality
  • Complements female hormonal optimization

Men’s Health

  • May support healthy testosterone levels
  • May reduce conversion of testosterone to estrogen
  • Supports male hormonal balance
  • Complements male vitality strategies

Breast Health

  • Emerging evidence for breast tissue health
  • Supports healthy estrogen metabolism
  • May reduce breast tissue changes
  • Complements breast health strategies

Dosage

Hormonal support: 100-150 mg daily Enhanced: 150-200 mg daily Full spectrum: 200-300 mg daily Timing: With meals containing fat (enhances absorption) Duration: 4-8 weeks for initial effects; 12+ weeks for full hormonal optimization

Best Forms

DIM Capsules (Recommended)

  • Standard form
  • Pre-dosed
  • Convenient
  • Effective

Research

Supports healthy estrogen metabolism and hormonal balance. Women’s health benefits documented. Detoxification support shown. Excellent safety profile.

Safety

Excellent safety profile. May cause minor detoxification symptoms initially (rare). Well-tolerated long-term.

Stacking

For women’s hormonal health: DIM 200 mg + Calcium D-Glucarate 500 mg + Magnesium 300 mg + Vitamin B6 25 mg

For detoxification: DIM 200 mg + NAC 1g + Milk Thistle 300 mg + Alpha Lipoic Acid 300 mg

For hormonal optimization: DIM 200 mg + Chaste Tree 400 mg + Vitex 400 mg + Magnesium 300 mg

Bottom Line

Cruciferous phytonutrient supporting healthy estrogen metabolism and hormonal balance for both men and women.

Key takeaways:

  • Supports healthy estrogen metabolism
  • Dose: 100-300 mg daily with fat
  • Results in 4-8 weeks; full benefits 12+ weeks
  • Excellent safety profile
  • Synergizes with calcium D-glucarate and NAC
  • Valuable for hormonal balance and detoxification

Important Warnings

Generally very safe. May cause minor detoxification symptoms initially. Pregnant women should consult doctor. Those with hormone-sensitive cancers should consult doctor.

Drug Interactions

Works synergistically with other estrogen metabolism supporters. No major interactions.
