TMG/Betaine (Trimethylglycine)

The methyl donor that supports cardiovascular health, detoxification, and cellular energy production

Research-Backed

Quick Facts

Typical Dosage 500-2000 mg
Best Time With meals
Best Form Capsules or powder
Results Timeline 4-8 weeks

When to Expect Results

Week 1-2

Initial absorption; methylation pathway activation

Week 2-4

Subtle improvements in energy and cardiovascular markers

Week 4-8

Noticeable improvements in homocysteine levels and vitality

Month 2+

Full cardiovascular and detoxification benefits realized

What it is: TMG (Trimethylglycine), also called betaine, is a methyl donor compound essential for methylation—a fundamental biochemical process underlying detoxification, energy production, and gene expression. TMG supplementation supports cardiovascular health via homocysteine reduction, cellular energy, and longevity pathways.

What Is TMG/Betaine?

TMG is a naturally occurring amino acid derivative found in beets, spinach, whole grains, and seafood. It contains three methyl groups (-CH3) that it donates to metabolic processes, making it a critical methyl donor in the body.

TMG serves as:

  • Methyl donor: Provides methyl groups for methylation reactions
  • Homocysteine reducer: Converts homocysteine to methionine (lowering cardiovascular risk)
  • Detoxification support: Methylation essential for Phase II detoxification
  • Energy production: Supports mitochondrial ATP and creatine synthesis

Benefits

Cardiovascular Health & Homocysteine Reduction

TMG’s primary benefit is cardiovascular support:

  • Reduces homocysteine levels 15-25% (independent cardiovascular risk factor)
  • Improves endothelial function
  • Supports blood pressure regulation
  • Reduces atherosclerotic risk markers
  • Results visible in 4-8 weeks

Detoxification Support

TMG enables Phase II methylation detoxification:

  • Many toxins require methylation for elimination
  • Enhanced liver detoxification capacity
  • Supports hormonal balance (estrogen metabolism)
  • Improved detoxification resilience

Cellular Energy Production

TMG supports ATP synthesis and creatine production:

  • Optimizes creatine synthesis (energy molecule)
  • Reduces fatigue in methylation-deficient individuals
  • Enhances exercise performance
  • Supports muscle recovery

Brain Health & Methylation

Adequate methylation essential for neurological function:

  • Supports neurotransmitter synthesis (dopamine, serotonin, acetylcholine)
  • Supports myelin formation (nerve insulation)
  • Reduces neuroinflammation
  • Supports cognitive function

Dosage Recommendations

Cardiovascular support: 500-1,000 mg daily

Enhanced detoxification/energy: 1,000-1,500 mg daily

Full spectrum: 1,500-2,000 mg daily (typically 500-1,000 mg twice daily)

Timing: With meals

Duration: 4-8 weeks for cardiovascular benefits

Note: Effectiveness enhanced when B vitamins (B6, B12, folate) also adequate.

Best Forms

TMG Capsules

  • Standard form, pre-dosed
  • Effective for most users

TMG Powder

  • More cost-effective
  • For long-term committed users

The Research

Cardiovascular Health: Multiple studies confirm 15-25% homocysteine reduction

Homocysteine Metabolism: TMG direct precursor to methionine; mechanism well-established

Energy & Athletic Performance: Supports creatine synthesis; improved power output documented

Detoxification: Methylation essential for Phase II; TMG provides methyl groups

Safety & Side Effects

Excellent safety profile:

  • Very rare mild nausea if taken without food
  • No serious adverse events documented

Stacking Recommendations

For cardiovascular health:

  • TMG 1,500 mg + Folate 400-800 mcg + Vitamin B12 1,000 mcg + Vitamin B6 25 mg

For methylation optimization:

  • TMG 1,500 mg + Choline 550 mg + SAMe 400 mg + Folate 400 mcg

For athletic performance:

  • TMG 1,500 mg + Creatine 5g + Beta-Alanine 3-5g + Carnosine 1,000 mg

Bottom Line

TMG is a foundational supplement that supports cardiovascular health and detoxification through methylation pathways. With strong evidence for homocysteine reduction and excellent safety, it’s valuable for cardiovascular optimization.

Key takeaways:

  • Methyl donor; converts homocysteine to methionine
  • Dose: 500-2,000 mg daily with meals
  • Results visible in 4-8 weeks
  • Synergizes with B vitamins
  • Valuable for cardiovascular health, detoxification, and energy

Important Warnings

Generally very safe. May increase homocysteine temporarily if folate/B12 deficient. Those with kidney disease should consult doctor. May interact with medications affecting methylation.

Drug Interactions

Works synergistically with B vitamins. Compatible with most supplements and medications.
