Red Yeast Rice

The traditional fermented rice that supports healthy cholesterol through natural statin-like compounds

Research-Backed

Quick Facts

Typical Dosage 1200-2400 mg
Best Time With meals
Best Form Capsules
Results Timeline 4-8 weeks

What it is: Red yeast rice is fermented rice containing naturally-occurring statins (monacolin compounds) that support healthy cholesterol levels. Used in traditional Chinese medicine for centuries, modern research confirms its cholesterol-lowering effects.

Benefits

Cholesterol Support

  • Reduces LDL cholesterol 15-30%
  • May increase HDL cholesterol
  • Supports cardiovascular lipid profiles
  • Natural alternative to pharmaceutical statins

Cardiovascular Health

  • Reduces cardiovascular disease risk
  • Anti-inflammatory in cardiovascular system
  • Supports arterial health
  • Antioxidant protection of lipids

CoQ10 Preservation

  • Unlike statins, may not deplete CoQ10
  • Supports mitochondrial function
  • Maintains cellular energy

Dosage

Cholesterol support: 1,200-1,500 mg daily Enhanced: 1,500-2,000 mg daily Maximum: 2,400 mg daily Timing: With meals containing fat Duration: 4-8 weeks for cholesterol effects

Best Forms

Red Yeast Rice Extract (Recommended)

  • Standardized to monacolin content
  • Consistent potency
  • Effective choice

Research

Multiple trials show 15-30% LDL reduction comparable to pharmaceutical statins. Natural compounds with similar mechanisms. Excellent safety record.

Safety

Generally safe. Very rare muscle discomfort (much less than pharmaceutical statins).

Stacking

Comprehensive cholesterol: Red Yeast Rice 1,500 mg + CoQ10 200 mg + Plant Sterols 2g + Omega-3 2g

Cardiovascular support: Red Yeast Rice 1,500 mg + Niacin 500 mg + Hawthorn 600 mg

Bottom Line

Red yeast rice naturally supports healthy cholesterol through monacolin compounds. Strong evidence for lipid support and natural statin alternative.

Key takeaways:

  • Natural statin-like cholesterol support
  • Dose: 1,200-2,400 mg daily with meals
  • Results in 4-8 weeks
  • Excellent safety profile
  • Synergizes with CoQ10, niacin, plant sterols
  • Natural alternative to pharmaceutical statins

Important Warnings

May cause mild muscle discomfort. Consult doctor if on statin drugs. Not for pregnant women.

Drug Interactions

May have additive cholesterol-lowering effects with statins.
